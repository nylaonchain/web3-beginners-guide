# Web3 Beginner's Guide

A simple introduction to Web3, crypto airdrops, and basic wallet safety.

## What is Web3?
To me, Web3 refers to a vision of the internet built around decentralized technologies, where users can interact with blockchain-based applications and have greater control over digital assets and identities.

## What is a Crypto Airdrop?

We can define a  crypto airdrop as a distribution of digital tokens or other rewards to eligible users. Projects may use airdrops to reward early users, contributors, or participants in specific activities.

## How Do Airdrops Work?

Eligibility varies from project to project. Some campaigns may involve completing quests, using a protocol, contributing to a community, or interacting with a blockchain application.

## Wallet Safety

Never share a wallet's seed phrase or private key with anyone. Before connecting a wallet to a website, verify that the website belongs to the legitimate project.

## Common Airdrop Scams

Scammers may create fake websites, impersonate projects, or promise guaranteed rewards. A request for your seed phrase or private key is a major warning sign.

## What I Learned

In my experience,Web3 opportunities can be interesting, but participation requires careful research. Understanding how wallets, blockchain applications, and airdrops work is important before taking part in campaigns.
